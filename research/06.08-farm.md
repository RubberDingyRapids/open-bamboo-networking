#### Two confusions to defuse

- **Port `8888` means two different things.** The `8888` in `research/08.08-print-abi.md:535` is **mitmproxy's** capture port in the recorded MITM workflow — unrelated to the farm REST `:8888` above (INTERESTING_REPOS.md:22).
- **Port `:1883` means two different things.** Plaintext printer MQTT on `:1883` beside TLS `:8883` is a normal-firmware LAN exposure (`research/06.02-mqtt.md:24`), distinct from farm mode where `:1883` is mutual-TLS and `:8883` is closed (INTERESTING_REPOS.md:22).
